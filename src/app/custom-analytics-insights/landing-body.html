Custom Conversion & Experiment Analysis

Custom Pricing · Based on Scope

Want to know whether a change to your store actually improved performance? We analyze before-and-after results, customer behavior, and performance data to measure the impact of changes, campaigns, or experiments.

  • Landing Page Test — Measure whether a new landing page improves conversion compared with the previous version.
  • Pricing Change Analysis — Determine whether changing a product’s price affected sales, AOV, or revenue.
  • Promotion Analysis — Measure whether a discount campaign actually generated incremental sales or simply reduced margins.
  • Checkout Improvement Analysis — Compare checkout performance before and after a UX change.
  • Campaign Impact Analysis — Determine whether a marketing campaign created meaningful improvement in revenue or customer acquisition.
