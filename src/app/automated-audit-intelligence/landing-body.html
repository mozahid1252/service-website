The Difference

What Is the Difference Between a Dashboard and Audit Intelligence?

Standard E-Commerce Intelligence Dashboard

A standard dashboard helps you see:
  • Revenue
  • Orders
  • Ad spend
  • ROAS
  • Conversion rate
  • Product sales
  • Customer metrics
  • Margin signals
  • Trends and comparisons
It mainly answers:
What is happening?

Automated Audit Intelligence

An intelligence module adds:
  • Audit-specific calculations
  • Business rules
  • Segmentation
  • Classifications
  • Performance-status labels
  • Threshold monitoring
  • Text-based insight summaries
  • Basic recommended actions
  • Selected business alerts
It helps answer:
What changed, why may it matter, and what should be reviewed next?
Simple example
Dashboard:
Meta ROAS decreased from 3.1x to 2.4x.
Audit Intelligence:
Meta spend increased while contribution-margin signals weakened.
Review recent budget concentration before increasing spend further.

The dashboard displays the metric. The intelligence module adds structured interpretation.

Insight Layer

Insight and Recommendation Layer

What the System Can Produce

Depending on the selected module, the system may produce short text summaries such as:

Performance Insight: Conversion rate declined during the selected period while traffic remained stable.
Business Signal: The decline is concentrated on mobile traffic and one landing-page group.
Recommended Review: Review mobile page experience and tracking consistency before increasing traffic acquisition.
Status: Review Priority: High Confidence: Moderate
Recommended insight format

Each insight can be structured as:

Observation: What changed?

Context: Where did the change occur?

Business relevance: Why may it matter?

Recommended next step: What should be reviewed or tested?

Confidence: How reliable is the signal?

This keeps the output useful without presenting automated recommendations as guaranteed decisions.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a full audit?
No. Automated Audit Intelligence is a recurring analytics and monitoring layer. It applies an agreed audit framework to available data and produces structured signals, insights, and basic recommendations.
Do I need an E-Commerce Intelligence Dashboard first?
Usually, yes. The module is designed to run on an existing or newly created data pipeline and dashboard environment.
Can I choose only one module?
Yes. You can start with one module, such as Marketing or Retention, and add another module later.
Can I monitor all modules together?
Yes, subject to data availability, dashboard scope, calculation requirements, and custom pricing.
Will the system write insight summaries?
Yes. The selected module may include text-based insight summaries generated from the configured calculations and rules.
Will it give recommendations?
It may provide basic recommendations such as Review, Monitor, Test, Maintain, Prioritize, or Investigate. These are decision-support signals, not guaranteed instructions.
Will it automatically make changes?
No. It will not change ad budgets, send campaigns, update prices, purchase inventory, or alter your website automatically unless a separate implementation system is created.
Will the dashboard update automatically?
Only if the Automated Scheduling, Refresh Monitoring & Error Alerts add-on is selected.
Will I receive alerts?
Relevant business alerts may be configured within the selected intelligence module. Technical refresh and failure alerts require the scheduling and monitoring add-on.
Can I change the rules later?
Yes. Rule updates, new KPIs, additional segments, and major dashboard changes may require additional scope.
Is this an AI system?
It is an automated analytics and decision-support system using defined calculations, business rules, monitoring conditions, and structured insight outputs. It should not be treated as an independent decision-maker.
Will it replace a data analyst?
It can reduce repetitive monitoring and organize recurring signals, but it does not replace human judgment, business context, or strategic decision-making.
Are cloud and software costs included?
No. Cloud hosting, software, API, and third-party platform costs are separate unless explicitly included in the proposal.
Is ongoing maintenance included?
No. Ongoing maintenance, rule updates, source changes, and technical support can be scoped separately.
