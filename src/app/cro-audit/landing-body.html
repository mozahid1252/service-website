Checkout Drop-Off Analysis

Objective: Remove Final-Step Friction

Data Analyzed: Checkout steps, form fields, payment flow, device performance, and other friction points.

User Journey Mapping

Objective: Understand How Visitors Navigate Your Site

Data Analyzed: The paths visitors take through your website, including entry pages, product views, key actions, and exit points.

We trace how visitors move through your site, where they engage, where they hesitate, and where they leave. This reveals navigation patterns and potential friction points that are difficult to identify from overall website metrics alone.

Example Insight: “70% of visitors entering through the homepage reach a product page, while only 15% of blog visitors continue to a product page. This suggests an opportunity to improve internal links and product discovery from content pages.”
Strategic Recommendation: Strengthen internal links, product recommendations, and calls-to-action on high-traffic pages to guide more visitors toward product pages and purchase-related actions.
