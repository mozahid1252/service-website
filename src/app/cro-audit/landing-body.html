Specialized Add-On · Package 04:E-Commerce CRO Audit

FIND WHAT IS PREVENTING YOUR VISITORS FROM BECOMING CUSTOMERS.

Your traffic may be growing, but conversion problems can exist across your landing pages, product pages, navigation, cart, checkout, offer, and mobile experience. The E-Commerce CRO Audit analyses the full customer journey and gives you a prioritized roadmap for improving conversion.

Audit My E-Commerce Conversion What's Covered
The Scope

What This Audit Helps Solve

Common conversion problems this audit is designed to diagnose.

Traffic vs Revenue Gap

Traffic is increasing, but revenue remains flat. Visitors view products but rarely add them to the cart.

Cart & Checkout Abandonment

High abandonment rates after add-to-cart or during checkout. Mobile conversion is significantly lower than desktop.

Unclear Priorities

Paid traffic reaches the store but does not convert. The internal team is unsure which conversion problem to fix first.

Message-Match Gaps

Marketing offers do not match the landing-page experience. Customers are uncertain about pricing, shipping, returns, or product quality.

Navigation Friction

Navigation makes product discovery difficult. Previous CRO changes were made without reliable data or testing.

The Outcome

After This Audit, You Will Know

Where customers are dropping off, whether the issue is traffic or experience, and what to improve first.

Where Customers Are Dropping Off

Understand how visitors move from traffic source to landing page, product page, cart, checkout, and completed purchase.

Which Funnel Stage Needs Attention First

Identify the highest-impact bottleneck instead of spending time on low-impact cosmetic changes.

Whether the Problem Is Traffic or Experience

Determine whether low conversion is related to traffic quality, messaging, product presentation, offer structure, or checkout friction.

Whether Your Pages Match Customer Intent

Find gaps between the campaign promise, landing page, product page, and checkout experience.

Which Devices and Segments Perform Poorly

Compare mobile, desktop, new visitors, returning visitors, traffic sources, campaigns, products, and geographic segments where reliable data is available.

What Should Be Improved First

Receive a prioritized roadmap based on potential impact, implementation effort, evidence quality, and testing requirements.

Experimentation & Prioritization Plan

Turn Findings Into Testable Improvements

Data Analyzed: Funnel findings, page observations, segment performance, revenue impact, implementation effort, and existing test history.

Every recommendation is structured around evidence and action. Each includes the problem, evidence, recommended change, hypothesis, expected impact, implementation effort, confidence level, suggested test method, primary success metric, and secondary metric.

Priority Categories:

  • Immediate Opportunities - High-impact changes that require limited technical effort.
  • High-Impact Improvements - Structural changes requiring design, copy, or development effort.
  • Testing Opportunities - Hypothesis-led changes that should be validated through A/B testing or another controlled method.
  • Monitoring Items - Metrics or issues that should be observed over time before further action.
Example Action: “Test a clearer delivery and returns message above the mobile add-to-cart button. Measure product-page-to-cart rate and completed-purchase rate for new mobile visitors.”

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Is this a website redesign service?
No. This is a data-informed CRO audit. It identifies what should be improved, why it matters, and how it could be tested. Design, copywriting, development, and implementation are scoped separately.
Does the audit cover the entire website?
The audit covers the primary conversion journey, including representative landing pages, product pages, navigation, cart, checkout, and mobile experience. A full site-wide audit requires a custom scope.
Do you guarantee a higher conversion rate?
No. Results depend on traffic quality, product-market fit, offer, pricing, implementation, seasonality, and testing.
Do you analyse landing pages and product pages?
Yes. The audit reviews page clarity, message match, product information, trust signals, offer presentation, CTAs, and conversion behaviour where reliable data is available.
Do you analyse cart and checkout?
Yes. We review cart-to-checkout progression, checkout completion, shipping visibility, payment options, device performance, and abandonment signals where data is available.
Can you analyse mobile conversion?
Yes. Mobile and desktop performance can be compared where device-level data is available.
Do you need heatmaps or session recordings?
No. They can be useful but are not mandatory. The audit can use Shopify, GA4, funnel data, page review, and available customer-behaviour data.
What if the tracking data is incomplete?
We document the limitation and reduce the confidence of the relevant recommendation. Major tracking repair can be scoped separately.
Does the audit include A/B testing?
The audit includes test hypotheses and an experimentation brief. Test setup, variant development, launch, and statistical analysis are separate services.
How long does the audit take?
The standard audit is delivered within 10-15 business days after the required data and access are available.
