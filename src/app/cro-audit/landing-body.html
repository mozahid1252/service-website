The Deep Dive

Six analyses that map exactly where revenue leaks out.

01. Full Sales Funnel Drop-Off Analysis

We map every step: Landing → Product → Cart → Checkout → Payment. We calculate the drop-off rate at every single transition.

02. Cart Abandonment Analysis

Analyze cart abandonment broken down by device, traffic source, category, and time of day. Patterns point directly to fixable causes.

03. Checkout Drop-Off Analysis

We examine every field, step, and friction point in your checkout flow. Often more severe than cart abandonment.

04. User Journey Mapping

Trace paths real visitors take — where they enter, hesitate, and exit. Reveals navigation problems invisible without data.

05. Bounce Rate & Engagement Analysis

Identify which pages visitors leave immediately. A high bounce rate from paid traffic is expensive. We'll show you why.

06. Mobile vs. Desktop Conversion Comparison

If mobile CR is significantly below desktop, you have a fixable problem. We identify precisely what is causing it.
