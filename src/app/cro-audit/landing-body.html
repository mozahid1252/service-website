Specialized Add-On · Package 04:Funnel & Conversion Audit

FIND EXACTLY WHERE YOUR SITE IS LEAKING SALES.

The average e-commerce conversion rate is 1.5% to 3%. If yours is below that, you're leaving revenue on the table from traffic you're already paying for. This audit maps every step of your customer's journey - from landing page to checkout.

Audit My Conversion Funnel What's Covered
The Scope

What This Audit Covers

Mapping exactly where revenue leaks out of your store.

The Purpose

To identify the exact pages and steps where visitors abandon the purchase journey and where the highest-impact conversion opportunity exists.

Problems Solved

Low conversion rates, high cart abandonment, weak mobile conversion, landing-page message gaps, and checkout friction.

Decisions Supported

UX redesigns, checkout flow simplification, message-match fixes, device-specific optimizations, and prioritized testing.

The Outcome

After This Audit, You Will Know

Where customers are dropping off, whether the issue is traffic or experience, and what to improve first.

Where Customers Are Dropping Off

Understand how visitors move from landing page to product page, cart, checkout, and purchase.

Which Funnel Stage Needs Attention First

Separate the highest-impact bottleneck from minor issues that may not materially affect revenue.

Whether the Problem Is Traffic or Experience

Identify whether low conversion is caused by traffic quality, weak messaging, product-page friction, offer problems, or checkout issues.

Whether Your Pages Match Customer Intent

Find gaps between the campaign promise and the landing-page or product-page experience.

Which Device or Segment Converts Poorly

Compare mobile, desktop, new visitors, returning visitors, traffic sources, campaigns, and products where reliable data is available.

What to Improve First

Receive a prioritized action plan based on potential impact, implementation effort, available evidence, and testing requirements.

Data Analyzed: Traffic source, campaign, landing page, sessions, engagement signals, conversion rate, revenue, and available customer-quality data.

We review paid, organic, direct, email, and referral traffic, conversion rate by source, landing-page performance, campaign-to-landing-page relationship, new versus returning visitors, mobile versus desktop traffic, high-volume but low-converting traffic, and traffic quality versus acquisition cost.

Example Insight: “Paid social generates strong landing-page traffic volume but a much lower product-view rate than branded search, suggesting a possible intent or message-match gap.”
Recommendation: Separate traffic-quality problems from on-page conversion problems before changing the page or increasing ad spend.

Cart & Checkout Friction Analysis

Objective: Identify Why Customers Abandon After Showing Purchase Intent

Data Analyzed: Add-to-cart events, cart sessions, checkout starts, payment attempts, completed purchases, device type, shipping cost, discount codes, and payment-method data where available.

We review add-to-cart-to-checkout progression, checkout-start-to-purchase completion, unexpected shipping costs, discount-code friction, payment-method availability, account-creation requirements, form complexity, mobile checkout experience, delivery-time uncertainty, trust or security concerns, and failed payment or technical error signals.

Example Insight: “Checkout initiation is strong, but completion drops sharply on mobile after shipping information appears. Shipping cost or delivery clarity should be investigated before changing the product offer.”

Conversion Opportunity & Experimentation Plan

Objective: Turn Findings Into Testable Improvements

Data Analyzed: Funnel findings, page observations, segment performance, revenue impact, implementation effort, and existing test history.

We prioritize high-impact friction points, high-volume funnel stages, low-converting pages, message-match opportunities, product-page improvements, checkout improvements, mobile experience improvements, offer and trust experiments, and measurement fixes required before testing. Each recommendation includes the problem, evidence, recommended change, expected impact, implementation effort, confidence level, suggested test method, and success metric.

Example Action: “Test a clearer delivery and returns message above the mobile add-to-cart button. Measure product-page-to-cart rate and completed-purchase rate for new mobile visitors.”
Recommendation: Use controlled testing where possible. If A/B testing is not feasible, prioritize changes using available evidence and monitor the relevant conversion metric.
Measurement Readiness Check: Before interpreting funnel performance, we verify whether the available events are sufficiently reliable. Missing funnel events, duplicate events, inconsistent purchase data, Shopify and GA4 alignment, and reliable add-to-cart, checkout, and purchase signals are checked. Major tracking repair is not included in this audit - it can be scoped separately through the E-Commerce Tracking & Attribution Review.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Is this a website redesign service?
No. This is a data-informed funnel and conversion audit. It identifies what should be improved and why. Design, copywriting, development, and implementation are scoped separately.
Do you guarantee a higher conversion rate?
No. The audit identifies evidence-based opportunities, but results depend on traffic quality, product-market fit, offer, pricing, implementation, seasonality, and testing.
Do you analyse landing pages?
Yes. We review landing-page performance, message match, clarity, offer presentation, trust signals, and conversion behaviour where reliable data is available.
Do you analyse checkout?
Yes. We review cart-to-checkout progression, checkout completion, device performance, shipping visibility, payment options, and abandonment signals where available.
Can you analyse mobile conversion?
Yes. Mobile and desktop performance can be compared where device-level data is available.
Do you need heatmaps or session recordings?
No. They can be useful, but they are not mandatory. The audit can use Shopify, GA4, funnel data, page review, and available customer-behaviour data.
What if the tracking data is incomplete?
We document the limitation and reduce the confidence of the relevant recommendation. Tracking repair can be scoped separately through the E-Commerce Tracking & Attribution Review.
How long does the audit take?
The standard audit is delivered within 7-10 business days after the required data and access are available.
