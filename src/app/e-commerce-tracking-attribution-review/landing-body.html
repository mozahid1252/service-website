Scope

What This Review Covers

To evaluate whether your Shopify, GA4, and advertising-platform tracking is capturing and attributing conversions consistently.

Problems Solved

  • Shopify, GA4, Meta, and Google numbers do not match
  • Purchase events are missing or duplicated
  • Marketing traffic is appearing as Direct or Unassigned
  • Pixel, GTM, or server-side events are unreliable
  • Ad platforms are reporting more or fewer conversions than the store
  • You do not know which data source to trust

Decisions Supported

  • Which data source should be used as the store-sales baseline
  • Whether your ad performance reports are inflated or incomplete
  • Which tracking issues need immediate correction
  • Whether your business is ready for marketing scaling
  • What your developer or agency should fix first

6 Marketing Measurement Analyses

1

Shopify, GA4 & Ad Platform Reconciliation

Objective: Explain Why Your Numbers Do Not Match

Data Analyzed: Shopify orders and revenue, GA4 purchases, Meta-attributed conversions, Google Ads conversions, refunds, date ranges, and currency settings.

We compare store-level orders and revenue with analytics and advertising-platform reports to identify missing, duplicate, unattributed, and differently attributed conversions.

Example Insight: “Shopify recorded 100 completed orders, while GA4 recorded 92 purchase events. Meta attributed 65 purchases to its advertising activity. Meta's 65 conversions should not be added to Shopify's 100 orders because platforms may claim credit for the same order.”
Strategic Recommendation: Define Shopify as the store-sales baseline and use GA4, Meta, and Google data for traffic, campaign, and attribution decisions according to their known limitations.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Who is this review for?
This review is designed for e-commerce brands using Shopify, GA4, Meta, Google Ads, or other marketing platforms whose reported orders, revenue, or conversions do not align.
What platforms are included?
The standard review covers your Shopify store, GA4, Google Tag Manager, and up to two major advertising or marketing platforms, such as Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, or Klaviyo. The exact platforms included depend on your tracking setup and business needs. We compare your store-level orders and revenue with the relevant analytics and advertising data to identify missing events, duplicate conversions, attribution differences, and tracking gaps. Additional platforms, multiple stores, complex server-side tracking, or multiple tracking environments may require an expanded scope and custom quote.
Will the numbers match exactly after the review?
Not necessarily. Different platforms use different attribution rules, windows, consent signals, and reporting logic. The goal is to explain the differences and make the data more reliable for each decision.
Does this include implementation?
No. The audit includes findings, recommendations, validation steps, and a developer/agency handoff roadmap. Implementation is available as a separate service.
Does this include ad management?
No. This is a measurement and attribution review, not a paid-media management service.
What data access is required?
Depending on scope, access may be required to Shopify, GA4, Google Tag Manager, Meta Events Manager, Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads, and existing tracking documentation.
How long does the review take?
The standard review is delivered within 7-10 business days after the required access and data are available.
