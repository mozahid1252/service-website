Phase 1 Deep Dive

6 Marketing Measurement Analyses

Every engagement begins with these six audits. Their findings drive the Phase 2 implementation and Phase 3 verification work that follows.

1

Shopify, GA4 & Ad Platform Reconciliation

Objective: Explain Why Your Numbers Do Not Match

Data Analyzed: Shopify orders and revenue, GA4 purchases, Meta-attributed conversions, Google Ads conversions, refunds, date ranges, and currency settings.

We compare store-level orders and revenue with analytics and advertising-platform reports to identify missing, duplicate, unattributed, and differently attributed conversions.

Example Insight: “Shopify recorded 100 completed orders, while GA4 recorded 92 purchase events. Meta attributed 65 purchases to its advertising activity. Meta's 65 conversions should not be added to Shopify's 100 orders because platforms may claim credit for the same order.”
Strategic Recommendation: Define Shopify as the store-sales baseline and use GA4, Meta, and Google data for traffic, campaign, and attribution decisions according to their known limitations.

Audit-Only vs End-to-End

An audit tells you what's broken. End-to-end fixes it and proves it works. Here's the difference.

Audit-Only Approach

You get a report

Findings and recommendations — then you find someone to implement them.

  • Tracking issues identified but left unfixed
  • Implementation handed off to a developer or agency

  • Delays between diagnosis and repair
  • Risk of fixes being implemented incorrectly

You get a working, verified system

Audit, implementation, and verification handled in one connected engagement.

  • Every tracking issue actually fixed, not just listed
  • GA4, GTM, Meta Pixel, CAPI & Google Ads configured live

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Who is this service for?
This service is designed for e-commerce brands using Shopify, GA4, Meta, Google Ads, or other marketing platforms whose reported orders, revenue, or conversions do not align — and who want the problem fixed end-to-end, not just diagnosed.
What platforms are included?
The standard scope covers your Shopify store, GA4, Google Tag Manager, and up to two major advertising or marketing platforms, such as Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, or Klaviyo. The exact platforms included depend on your tracking setup and business needs. Additional platforms, multiple stores, complex server-side tracking, or multiple tracking environments may require an expanded scope and custom quote.
Does this include implementation?
Yes. This is an end-to-end service. Phase 1 audits your tracking, Phase 2 implements all the fixes (GA4, GTM, Meta Pixel, Meta CAPI, Google Ads conversion tracking, UTM standardization, and duplicate-event correction), and Phase 3 verifies everything with test purchases. You receive a working, verified tracking stack — not just a report.
Will the numbers match exactly after the service?
Not necessarily — and that is expected. Different platforms use different attribution rules, windows, consent signals, and reporting logic, so they will never be forced to show the exact same number. What this service guarantees is that your tracking is technically correct, deduplication is in place, and you understand exactly why the remaining differences exist. Shopify will be your reliable store-sales baseline, and every other platform will be trustworthy for its intended decision.
Does this include ad management?
No. This is a measurement and attribution implementation service, not a paid-media management service. We make your data trustworthy so your media decisions — and your media manager — can be trusted.
What data access is required?
Depending on scope, access may be required to Shopify admin, GA4, Google Tag Manager, Meta Events Manager, Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads, your website theme or checkout, and existing tracking documentation. Implementation in Phase 2 may require collaborator or edit access to GTM, GA4, and your store theme.
How long does the full service take?
The standard end-to-end engagement is delivered within 3–4 weeks (15–20 business days) after the required access and data are available: Phase 1 audit in 3–5 days, Phase 2 implementation in 7–10 days, and Phase 3 testing & verification in 3–5 days.
What if my setup is more complex than standard?
Standard scope covers Shopify plus GA4 and up to two major marketing platforms. Complex multi-platform environments, custom server-side tracking, headless storefronts, multi-store setups, or migration from a legacy tag setup may require an expanded scope and a custom quote. We will confirm the exact scope after the Phase 1 audit.
