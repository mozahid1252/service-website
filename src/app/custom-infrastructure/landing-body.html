Chapter 04 · Important Notes

You only pay for what you actually use.

Four principles that govern how we scope, price, and deliver every setup engagement.

01

You only pay for what you actually use.

If your business runs on Shopify and Google Ads only, we connect those two platforms. You are never charged for platforms you don’t use.

02

Platform connections are charged per platform.

The Data Pipeline Build (Item 7) is priced per platform. A client using 3 platforms pays for 3 pipelines. A client using 5 pays for 5. No flat-rate surprises.

03

Automation is only for ongoing clients.

Items 12, 13, and 14, scheduling, alerts, and security, are only relevant if your pipeline needs to run repeatedly over time. One-time analysis clients do not need these.

04

Complexity affects the final price.

The ranges reflect real variability. A store with 500 SKUs and 3 years of historical data requires more work than a store with 50 SKUs and 6 months of data. Final pricing is confirmed after a brief discovery conversation.

Pick your services or let us help you map your stack to the right setup. Final pricing is confirmed after a brief discovery conversation.
