Specialized Add-On · Package 01: Marketing Performance & Profitability Audit

Stop Guessing Where Your Marketing Budget Is Going

Understand which marketing channels are creating profitable growth, where efficiency is weakening, and what deserves attention next.

Your platform may report an acceptable ROAS while your actual business profit tells a different story. Discounts, product costs, customer acquisition costs, refunds, attribution differences, and repeat-purchase value can all change the real outcome. The Marketing Performance & Profitability Audit connects your marketing spend with available revenue, cost, customer, and conversion data to provide a clearer view of channel efficiency and marketing profitability.

Audit My Marketing Performance See the Warning Signs

This is a focused marketing and acquisition audit. It is not a full business profitability audit, daily ad-management service, or campaign implementation engagement.

Warning Signs

Is Your Marketing Showing These Signs?

If several of these feel familiar, your marketing reporting is not telling you the full story.

  • ROAS is declining or becoming unpredictable
  • Revenue is increasing, but profit is not
  • Meta, Google, GA4, and Shopify report different results
  • You are unsure which channels are genuinely efficient
  • You do not know your real CAC by channel
  • Returning customers are being credited to acquisition campaigns
  • Some campaigns generate revenue but may not generate sufficient profit
  • Budget is concentrated in channels showing weaker efficiency
  • You are increasing spend without knowing where the next dollar should go
  • You need a clear marketing budget reallocation plan
The Outcome

What This Audit Helps You Understand

A clearer view of marketing efficiency, acquisition economics, and budget priorities.

Which marketing channels show stronger or weaker efficiency

How platform-reported performance differs from available business data

Where CAC appears high or sustainable

Which channels attract new customers and which mainly support returning customers

How available customer-value signals affect acquisition decisions

Which campaigns or campaign groups deserve further review

Where budget may be concentrated inefficiently

Whether certain periods or channels show diminishing-return signals

Which traffic sources bring stronger conversion or customer-quality signals

What should be scaled, maintained, tested, reduced, or investigated next

Note

The recommendations will be based on the available evidence. They will not be presented as guaranteed outcomes.

Analysis

What We Analyze

Six connected analyses covering channel efficiency, acquisition economics, campaign performance, spend distribution, traffic quality, and budget direction.

01

Channel Performance & Profitability Signals

Objective: Understand the Business Contribution of Each Marketing Channel

We compare available channel-level spend, revenue, conversion, customer, and cost data rather than relying only on the ROAS reported inside an advertising platform.

We may review:

  • Meta, Google, Email, Organic, Direct, Referral, and other available sources
  • Channel spend
  • Reported revenue
  • Shopify or e-commerce-platform revenue
  • New-customer contribution
  • Returning-customer contribution
  • Available product-cost and margin signals
  • Attribution limitations

You will learn:

  • Which channels show stronger efficiency signals
  • Which channels generate revenue without clear profitability evidence
  • Which channels require improved measurement
  • Where channel-level budget review may be appropriate
Note: True profitability depends on the quality and completeness of product cost, discount, refund, return, shipping, payment, and other relevant cost data.
02

Customer Acquisition Cost, LTV Signals & Payback

Objective: Connect Acquisition Cost With Available Customer Value

We compare available customer acquisition costs with customer-value signals to understand whether channel performance appears sustainable beyond the first purchase.

We may review:

  • Ad spend
  • Platform fees (where available)
  • Creative or production costs (where available)
  • New customers by channel
  • CAC signals
  • Repeat-purchase signals
  • Customer-value or LTV signals
  • Time to second purchase (where available)
  • Available CAC payback signals
  • Acquisition quality by channel

You will learn:

  • Which channels acquire customers at a higher or lower apparent cost
  • Which channels appear to attract higher-value customers
  • Whether a channel's initial CAC may be supported by later customer value
  • Which customer-value questions require deeper retention or LTV analysis
Note: Full predictive LTV modeling, churn prediction, and retention implementation are not included in this audit.
03

Campaign Performance Review

Objective: Identify Campaign Groups That Deserve Further Attention

Where campaign-level data is available and the volume is sufficient, we review active campaigns or campaign groups to identify performance patterns.

We may review:

  • Campaign spend
  • Campaign revenue
  • Reported ROAS
  • Conversion volume
  • CAC signals
  • New-customer contribution
  • Product or offer differences
  • Campaign trend
  • Available audience or objective information

Campaign directions may include:

  • Scale carefully
  • Maintain
  • Investigate
  • Test
  • Reduce
  • Pause for review
Note: We do not automatically recommend killing a campaign based on one isolated metric or a short performance period. Recommendations consider data quality, learning phase, attribution limitations, spend level, margin, and customer value. Detailed creative-by-creative, ad-set-by-ad-set, audience, placement, and media-buying analysis may require a separate Meta Ads or paid-media specialist audit.
04

Marketing Spend Efficiency

Objective: Understand How Effectively the Available Budget Is Being Used

We review how spend is distributed across available channels, campaigns, products, and time periods.

We may review:

  • Spend concentration
  • Revenue concentration
  • CAC by channel
  • ROAS by channel
  • Conversion efficiency
  • New-customer acquisition
  • Product or offer mix
  • Period-over-period performance
  • Available spend-to-margin signals
  • Budget dependency and concentration risk

You will learn:

  • Where budget concentration may create risk
  • Which areas deserve more investigation
  • Where spend may be inefficient relative to available business outcomes
  • Whether a reallocation test may be appropriate
05

Traffic Source & Conversion Quality

Objective: Compare Traffic Quality With Acquisition Economics

A high-converting source is not automatically the most profitable source. We compare conversion signals with acquisition cost and customer-value signals where the data supports it.

We may review:

  • Sessions or visits by source
  • Conversion rate
  • Purchases
  • Revenue
  • New versus returning visitors
  • New-customer contribution
  • Device or landing-page differences (where available)
  • Traffic-source quality
  • CAC and margin signals

You will learn:

  • Which sources appear to convert more efficiently
  • Which sources bring traffic but require investigation
  • Whether high conversion is accompanied by valuable customers
  • Whether a source needs a deeper CRO, tracking, or retention review
06

Budget Reallocation & Scenario Planning

Objective: Create a Practical Marketing Investment Direction

We translate the findings into a prioritized budget recommendation. This is not a guaranteed forecast. It is a decision framework based on available historical performance and business constraints.

We may provide:

  • Current budget distribution
  • Recommended budget priorities
  • Areas to protect
  • Areas to test
  • Areas to reduce or review
  • Conservative reallocation scenarios
  • Small-test recommendations
  • Measurement requirements
  • Risk and confidence notes

The recommendation may show:

  • Where to increase carefully
  • Where to maintain current investment
  • Where to reduce exposure
  • Where to run a controlled test
  • Which metric should determine the next decision
Note: Marginal-return modeling requires sufficient historical spend and conversion data. Where this data is not available, we provide directional budget recommendations instead.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this audit for?
It is best suited for e-commerce brands with consistent paid-marketing activity that need to understand channel efficiency, acquisition economics, and marketing budget priorities.
Is this a full Meta Ads audit?
No. This audit provides a broader marketing-performance and profitability review. A dedicated Meta Ads Audit can examine account structure, campaign setup, audience, creative, placements, and platform-specific issues in greater detail.
Is this a full business profitability audit?
No. It focuses on marketing performance and acquisition economics. A full E-Commerce Profitability Audit reviews broader product, cost, discount, refund, return, and business-level profitability.
Will you tell me exactly which campaigns to kill?
We may recommend reducing, pausing, or investigating specific campaigns when the evidence supports that direction. We do not make irreversible recommendations from a single metric, short time period, or unreliable attribution data.
Is the ROAS shown in this audit the same as platform-reported ROAS?
Not necessarily. We compare reported metrics with available store, analytics, customer, and cost data and explain the relevant limitations.
Can you calculate true profit by channel?
We can estimate channel-level profitability signals when reliable spend, product-cost, discount, refund, return, shipping, payment, and customer data are available. If important cost data is missing, the finding will be labeled as limited or directional.
Can you calculate LTV and CAC payback?
We can review LTV and CAC-payback signals where sufficient customer-level history and acquisition-source data are available. Full predictive LTV modeling is not included in the base audit.
Will you reallocate my ad budget?
You will receive a budget reallocation recommendation. Actual budget changes, campaign management, and media buying are not included.
Will you fix the campaigns?
No. The audit identifies priorities and recommendations. Campaign implementation is a separate service.
What if my advertising data and Shopify data do not match?
We document the differences, investigate the likely causes where possible, and recommend which source should guide each type of decision.
Can I add an automated dashboard?
Yes. An automated dashboard and data pipeline can be scoped separately through the Automate Your Data Infrastructure & Dashboards service.
How long does the audit take?
The audit is typically delivered within 5–7 business days after the required access and data are received.
