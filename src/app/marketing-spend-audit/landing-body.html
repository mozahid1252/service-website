Customer Acquisition: LTV, CAC & Payback

Objective: Understand Real Acquisition Costs & Customer Value

Data Analyzed: Ad spend, platform fees, creative costs, customer acquisition data, and customer value by channel.

We calculate the true cost of acquiring customers across each marketing channel, compare acquisition costs against customer lifetime value, and measure how quickly those costs are recovered.

Example Insight: “Facebook customers cost $41 to acquire and generate an LTV ratio of 2.1:1, while Google customers cost $34 and generate a 3.8:1 ratio. Google also recovers acquisition costs in just 2.8 months.”
Strategic Recommendation: Scale channels with sustainable LTV ratios and faster CAC payback, while reducing or capping spend where acquisition costs are too high relative to customer value.

Ad Spend Efficiency Analysis

Conversion Rate by Traffic Source

Objective: Understand Traffic Quality

Data Analyzed: Conversion rates, sessions, and purchases broken down by traffic source and channel.

Not all traffic converts equally. We compare how visitors from organic, paid, direct, email, referral, and other sources move from visit to purchase, helping identify which sources bring higher-quality traffic.

Example Insight: “Organic traffic converts at 3.0%, compared with 0.8% from paid traffic. However, paid traffic generates significantly more qualified customers in specific campaigns. Here’s where traffic quality and acquisition efficiency need to be evaluated together.”
