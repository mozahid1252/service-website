    Is Your Business Data Difficult to Monitor?

    This setup may be useful if:

    • Your data is spread across multiple platforms
    • You manually export reports every week or month
    • Shopify, GA4, Meta, and Google Ads show different numbers
    • Your team cannot see sales, marketing, product, and customer data together
    • Monthly reporting takes too much time
    • You rely on disconnected spreadsheets
    • You want one dashboard for management visibility
    • You need a structured data foundation for recurring reporting
    • You want to reduce repetitive data preparation
    • You need clearer visibility before making business decisions
