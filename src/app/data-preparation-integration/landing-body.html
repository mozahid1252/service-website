When You Need It

When You May Need This Service

Data Preparation & Integration may be useful if:

  • Your business data is spread across several platforms
  • You rely on manual CSV exports or spreadsheets
  • Shopify, GA4, Meta, and Google show different numbers
  • Product, customer, marketing, or profitability data is not organized
  • Your audit requires data from multiple sources
  • You are unsure whether your data is complete or consistent
  • You want to avoid repeatedly preparing the same data for future analysis
  • You need a clean dataset before starting a Health Check or specialist audit

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this an audit?
No. This service prepares and organizes data for an audit, Health Check, report, or custom analysis. The actual analysis and recommendations are separate.
Does “up to 5 platforms” apply to both options?
Yes. Both Specialist Audit Data Preparation and Health Check Data Preparation can include up to five standard platforms or data sources, subject to the agreed scope and data complexity.
What is the difference between the two options?
Specialist Audit Data Preparation prepares data for one focused audit. Health Check Data Preparation covers a broader set of business data required to review multiple areas of an e-commerce business.
Can you connect Shopify, GA4, Meta, and Google Ads?
Yes, where the required access is available and the data can be accessed through standard platform connections or files.
Will you combine all my data into one complete data warehouse?
Not by default. This service prepares data for the agreed audit or analysis. A full data warehouse, unified data model, dashboard, or automated data system requires a separate scope.
Will you fix mismatched numbers between platforms?
We can perform basic reconciliation and document important differences. Advanced attribution modelling or complete tracking correction is not included by default.
Can I use the prepared data for another audit later?
The prepared data may be reusable if the structure and historical period are suitable. Additional preparation may be required for a different audit or analysis objective.
Is the data preparation fee included in the audit price?
It depends on the proposal. Data preparation may be listed separately or bundled with a selected audit. The final proposal will clearly show the audit fee, preparation fee, and total investment.
Do you provide a dashboard?
No. Dashboard setup is a separate service under Automate Your Data Infrastructure & Dashboards.
How long does preparation take?
Specialist Audit Data Preparation typically takes 2–5 business days. Health Check Data Preparation typically takes 5–10 business days, depending on data access and complexity.
