Turn Your Health Check Findings Into Measurable Improvements

Your Health Check identifies where revenue, profitability, conversion, marketing efficiency, customer value, or data quality may be holding your business back. The E-Commerce Performance Improvement engagement turns those findings into a focused improvement plan and executes the highest-priority work required to strengthen your business performance.

Every engagement is custom-built around your Health Check results, business goals, available resources, and technical requirements.

Prioritization

How the Work Is Prioritized

Each possible improvement is assessed against a consistent set of criteria so that the highest-value work is selected first.

Each possible improvement is assessed using:

  • Business impact
  • Urgency
  • Evidence quality
  • Implementation effort
  • Technical complexity
  • Dependency on other work
  • Available internal resources
  • Ability to measure the outcome

Priority Categories

  • Immediate ImprovementsHigh-priority issues that can be addressed quickly.
  • High-Impact ImprovementsStructural changes that may require design, development, or technical support.
  • Measurement ImprovementsTracking or reporting work required before performance can be evaluated reliably.
  • Testing OpportunitiesChanges that should be validated through an experiment.
  • Future OpportunitiesUseful improvements that are not the first priority.

Process

How It Works

Six steps from Health Check findings to a measured, reviewed improvement outcome.

1

Review the Health Check

Review Standard or Deep Health Check findings and identify issues with strongest business impact.

2

Select the Workstream

Determine whether the project should focus on CRO, Marketing, Product profitability, Tracking, Retention, Reporting, or a combination.

3

Define the Scope

Receive a custom proposal showing work, deliverables, timeline, access, success metrics, fees, and exclusions.

4

Execute the Approved Work

Selected improvements are implemented or coordinated according to the agreed scope.

5

Measure the Result

Relevant KPIs are reviewed against the original baseline where data and timing allow.

6

Decide the Next Phase

Complete another focused improvement, move into Growth Intelligence Partnership, continue internally, or pause and monitor.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to complete a Health Check first?
Usually, yes. The Health Check provides the evidence needed to create an efficient custom scope. If you already have a recent, reliable audit or equivalent analysis, we can review it before deciding whether a new Health Check is necessary.
Is this a fixed package?
No. E-Commerce Performance Improvement is a custom engagement based on the problems identified and the work required.
Will you fix every issue found in the Health Check?
Not automatically. We select the highest-priority issues based on impact, effort, dependencies, and available budget. Additional issues can be addressed in later phases.
Do you implement the changes directly?
Implementation depends on the approved scope. MozIntelligence may provide strategy, documentation, coordination, analysis, and selected implementation. Developer, designer, copywriter, media buyer, or email-specialist work may require separate resources.
Is CRO implementation included?
Only if CRO work is included in the approved project scope.
Is campaign management included?
No, not by default. Daily media buying and campaign management require a separate scope.
Is a dashboard included?
No. KPI planning or reporting recommendations may be included. Dashboard development and automated data infrastructure are separately scoped.
Do you guarantee growth?
No. The engagement is designed to improve decision quality and address identified performance constraints. Results depend on implementation, traffic, product-market fit, pricing, market conditions, and other factors.
Can the work be delivered in phases?
Yes. A phased approach is recommended when multiple problems are connected or the project is technically complex.
How long does a project take?
A focused sprint typically takes 2–4 weeks. Multi-workstream projects generally take 4–8 weeks. The final timeline is confirmed in the custom proposal.
What happens after the project?
You may continue with another improvement phase or move into the E-Commerce Growth Intelligence Partnership for ongoing monitoring, strategic planning, and continuous optimization.
