The Engineering Process

What Happens During This Setup

A simple, structured process to connect your business data, organize it properly, and prepare it for reliable analysis.

1. Understand Your Data

We review your current platforms and identify which data sources are needed.

2. Check Platform Access

We confirm that the required data can be accessed safely from each platform.

3. Set Up Your Data Environment

We create the required cloud database environment where your business data can be stored securely.

4. Connect Your Platforms

We securely connect platforms such as Shopify, GA4, and advertising accounts to the data environment.

5. Prepare the Data Flow

We set up the required data flow so information from your connected platforms can be collected and organized for analysis.

6. Clean & Check the Data

We remove errors, standardize the data, and verify that the numbers are accurate and complete.

7. Ready for Analysis

You receive a clean, organized dataset that is ready for audits, dashboards, reporting, and future analysis.

What We Build

Every component of the Basic Setup is designed to create a reliable foundation for your business data and future analysis.

1. Cloud Infrastructure Setup

Purpose: Create the central home for your business data.

What We Configure: A Google Cloud Platform (GCP) project, billing configuration, and BigQuery database.

Why It Matters: Provides a secure and scalable cloud data warehouse for your business data. Instead of relying on local files or scattered spreadsheets, your data is stored in a structured cloud environment.

# GCP Infrastructure
project_id = "moz-client-prod"

2. API Connection

Purpose: Establish a secure connection to your business data.

What We Configure: Secure API authentication, access credentials, and the required permission scopes for each connected platform.

Why It Matters: Allows us to securely retrieve relevant orders, customers, products, analytics, or other required business data without unnecessarily exposing or modifying your platform.

3. Data Pipeline Build (Per Platform)

Purpose: Automate the extraction of your business data.

What We Configure: Python-based extraction scripts that securely pull required data from your connected platforms on demand. Includes 2–3 platforms.

Why It Matters: Replaces repetitive manual CSV exports with a reproducible data pipeline, improving consistency, reliability, and efficiency.

4. Data Cleaning & Standardization

Purpose: Make data from different sources consistent and analysis-ready.

What We Configure: Standardization of column names, currencies, date formats, timezones, and other relevant data fields.

Why It Matters: Raw data often contains inconsistencies. Cleaning and standardization ensures your data is accurate, consistent, and ready for reliable analysis.

5. Data Integration & Platform Preparation (2–3 Platforms)

Purpose: Prepare data from your connected platforms for analysis.

What We Configure: We organize and prepare data from your connected platforms, including the required fields, formats, and data structures needed for analysis.

Why It Matters: Your data is prepared in a consistent structure so the required analysis can be performed accurately.

Complex multi-platform data merging and unified data modeling are NOT included in the Basic Setup and are available with the Full Pipeline Setup.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Does this include automated daily updates?
No. The Basic Setup is designed for one-time analysis engagements. It pulls your data once, cleans it, and prepares it for immediate use. If you need daily automated updates, you should consider the Full Pipeline + Automation bundle.
Which platforms can you connect?
We can connect platforms such as Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, GA4, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Klaviyo, and other relevant business data sources. This Basic Setup includes 2–3 platforms of your choice. If you need multiple platforms fully merged into a unified data environment, please look at the Full Pipeline Setup.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely. We use read-only API access and follow strict security protocols. We do not store your credentials in plain text, and we never modify your live business data.
