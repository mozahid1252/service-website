Demand Forecasting Analysis

Objective: Predict Future Inventory Needs

Data Analyzed: Historical sales velocity, seasonality, recent demand trends, and current inventory levels.

We build a 90-day demand forecast for your key products to help you plan purchasing based on expected demand rather than guesswork. We identify products at risk of stockouts and products where inventory may exceed expected demand.

Example Insight: “Product B is expected to sell approximately 500 units over the next 90 days, while current inventory covers only around 350 units of projected demand. Without a timely reorder, the product may face a stockout.”
Strategic Recommendation: Reorder Product B within 10 days to maintain sufficient stock and reduce the risk of a stockout during the next demand cycle.

Inventory Efficiency Analysis

Objective: Free Up Cash Tied Up in Inventory

Data Analyzed: Sell-through rates, days of inventory on hand, inventory turnover, and stock value by product.

We identify which products are moving quickly and which products are tying up cash unnecessarily. This shows where inventory is healthy, where reordering should continue, and where purchasing should be reduced or paused.

Example Insight: “Product A turns over quickly with only 15 days of inventory on hand, while Product B has 120 days of stock available. The slow-moving inventory is tying up significantly more cash than necessary.”
Strategic Recommendation: Pause reorders for SKUs with more than 90 days of inventory on hand and prioritize actions that improve sell-through of slow-moving stock.

Dead Stock Analysis

Objective: Clear the Warehouse and Recover Tied-Up Cash

Data Analyzed: Products with zero or minimal sales movement over a defined period, including inventory value, storage costs, and recovery potential.

We identify products that are no longer generating meaningful sales and calculate the cost of keeping them in inventory. We then estimate how much cash can realistically be recovered through liquidation, bundles, or targeted clearance offers.

Example Insight: “$12K of inventory has generated little to no movement, creating $1.2K in estimated holding costs. A targeted liquidation strategy could recover approximately $800 while freeing valuable warehouse space.”
Strategic Recommendation: Liquidate dead stock through bundle deals with A-tier products, targeted clearance offers, or other recovery strategies before additional holding costs accumulate.

Order Fulfillment Analysis

Objective: Reduce Order Processing and Dispatch Time

Data Analyzed: Processing time from purchase to dispatch by product, supplier, order volume, and fulfillment stage.

We analyze how long orders take to move from purchase to dispatch and identify where delays occur. This helps uncover operational bottlenecks that can slow delivery, increase support requests, and negatively affect customer satisfaction.

Example Insight: “Orders are currently taking an average of 48 hours to reach dispatch, twice the 24-hour target. The delay is concentrated in manual order processing and shipping label preparation.”
Strategic Recommendation: Automate shipping label generation and streamline order processing to reduce dispatch time by approximately 24 hours and move fulfillment closer to the target.

Return & Refund Operations Analysis

Objective: Reduce Return Costs and Improve Operational Efficiency

Data Analyzed: Return rates, refund amounts, reverse shipping costs, restocking costs, and lost margin by product, category, and supplier.

We identify which products and categories generate the most returns and calculate the true operational cost behind them. This reveals whether returns are creating hidden margin loss and which specific issues are driving customers to send products back.

Example Insight: “Product A has a 15% return rate compared with just 3% for Product B. With 150 returns from Product A, reverse shipping, restocking, and lost margin are creating approximately $3.6K in avoidable costs.”
Strategic Recommendation: Investigate the root causes behind Product A's higher return rate and improve product information, sizing guidance, quality control, or supplier performance to reduce avoidable returns.
