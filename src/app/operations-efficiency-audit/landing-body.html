The Deep Dive

Eight analyses that quantify every operational leak.

01

Demand Forecasting Analysis

90-day forward-looking demand projection for top products. Purchasing decisions driven by data. Flag stockout risks and overstock.

02

Inventory Efficiency Analysis

Calculate sell-through rates, days of inventory on hand, and turnover. Identify where cash is tied up in slow-moving stock.

03

Dead Stock Analysis

Identify unmoved products, calculate true holding costs (storage, opportunity, obsolescence), and recommend liquidation strategies.

04

Order Fulfillment Analysis

Analyze processing time from purchase to dispatch by product, supplier, and volume. Fix delays causing negative reviews.

05

Delivery & Shipping Performance

Examine delivery time, reliability, and cost as % of revenue by region/carrier. Find disproportionate costs and satisfaction problems.

06

Return & Refund Operations Analysis

Analyze return rates by product/category/supplier. Calculate total cost (reverse shipping, restocking, lost margin). Identify root causes.

07

Supplier Performance Analysis

Build a scorecard for every supplier: on-time delivery, order accuracy, defect rate, cost consistency.

08

Geographic Sales & Fulfillment

Identify which regions generate revenue, which generate returns, and where fulfillment is weakest. Inform carrier and warehouse decisions.
