ABC Product Classification

Objective: Identify Your Most and Least Valuable Products

Data Analyzed: Revenue contribution and sales performance of each SKU.

We classify every product in your catalog into three tiers based on its contribution to your business: A-Tier products that drive the most value, B-Tier products that need optimization, and C-Tier products that contribute the least and may need to be reduced or discontinued.

Example Insight: “Your A-Tier products generate the majority of revenue, while many C-Tier products contribute little but continue to tie up inventory and cash.”
Strategic Recommendation: Protect and grow high-value A-Tier products, optimize B-Tier products, and review C-Tier products for potential reduction or discontinuation.

Product-Level Profit Margin Analysis

Objective: Find Your True Product Profitability

Data Analyzed: COGS, shipping costs, return costs, discounts, and proportional marketing costs per product.

We calculate the true profit margin of each product after accounting for the costs that are often missed when looking at revenue alone. This reveals which products are genuinely profitable and which top sellers may be quietly reducing your overall profit.

Example Insight: “Product A generates strong revenue but has only a 5% true profit margin, while Product B delivers a 45% margin. Your highest-selling products aren't always your most profitable.”
Strategic Recommendation: Reprice, bundle, or reduce marketing spend on low-margin products to protect and improve overall profitability.

Dead Stock Identification

Objective: Free Up Cash Tied Up in Slow-Moving Products

Data Analyzed: Products with little or no sales movement over a defined period, along with their inventory value and holding costs.

We identify products that are no longer selling and calculate how much cash is tied up in them. We then estimate how much value could potentially be recovered through discounts, bundles, promotions, or other liquidation strategies.

Example Insight: “$1.2K is currently tied up in products with little or no movement. We estimate that approximately $800 could be recovered through targeted clearance and bundling strategies.”
Strategic Recommendation: Bundle dead stock with high-performing products, run targeted clearance offers, or discontinue products that are unlikely to recover their costs.

Product Return & Refund Analysis

Objective: Identify Products Losing Profit Through Returns

Data Analyzed: Product-level return rates, refund amounts, return reasons, and revenue impact.

We identify which products generate unusually high returns and refunds, and measure how much revenue and profit those returns are costing your business. This helps separate products that sell well from products that actually deliver profitable sales.

Example Insight: “Product A has a 14% return rate, significantly higher than the 5% portfolio average. Returns from this product are reducing its actual profitability despite strong sales.”

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Who is this audit for?
Stores with large product catalogs (50+ SKUs), high inventory investment, or plans to expand/rationalize their product line.
What data access is required?
We need read-only access to Shopify admin (products, orders, inventory) and your COGS (Cost of Goods Sold) data to calculate true margins.
How long does the audit take?
The audit is completed within 7,14 business days, depending on catalog size.
Will implementation be included?
Implementation is not included, but we provide a 45-minute strategy call and 2 weeks of email support to guide your catalog restructuring.
Is the automated dashboard included in the package?
No. The automated dashboard is not included in the standard Product Portfolio Audit package. It is an optional upgrade and must be purchased separately.
