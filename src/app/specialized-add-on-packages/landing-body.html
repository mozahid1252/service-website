Package 01 / 06

Marketing Spend Audit

"I'm spending on ads every month — but my ROAS is declining and I genuinely don't know where the problem is."

You're running Facebook ads, Google ads, maybe email campaigns too. The money goes out every month — but the returns feel less predictable than they used to. Your ROAS numbers look acceptable on the surface, but your actual profit tells a different story. You're not sure whether to spend more, spend less, or spend differently. This audit gives you the answer.

Investment$300 — $600
Timeline7–10 Days
Best For$3K+/mo ad spend

What We Analyze

01

Channel Performance Breakdown

Every marketing channel examined side by side — Facebook, Google, Email, Organic, Direct. We calculate the true revenue contribution of each, not just the last-click attribution number your ad platform shows you.

02

True Cost Per Acquisition (CAC) by Channel

We calculate what it actually costs to acquire one paying customer through each channel — including ad spend, platform fees, and creative costs. This number is almost always different from what the ad dashboard reports.

03

Campaign-Level ROI Analysis

We go beyond account-level performance and examine individual campaigns, ad sets, and offers. We identify which specific campaigns are profitable, which are breaking even, and which are actively losing money.

04

Ad Spend Efficiency Analysis

We analyze how your budget is distributed across channels, campaigns, and time periods — and identify where budget concentration is working against you.

05

Conversion Rate by Traffic Source

Not all traffic converts equally. We analyze conversion rates broken down by the source of the visitor — because a 3% conversion rate from organic traffic and a 0.8% rate from paid traffic tells a very important story about where your money should go.

Deliverables & Promise

Marketing Audit Report (6–8 pages)
Channel-by-channel breakdown with findings written for decision-makers.
True ROAS vs. Reported ROAS Comparison
Side-by-side view of what your dashboards say vs. what the data actually shows.
Budget Reallocation Plan
Specific recommendation: where to increase, where to cut, and by how much.
Campaign Performance Scorecard
Every active campaign rated: Scale / Maintain / Pause / Kill.
45-Minute Strategy Call + 2 Weeks Email Support

After this audit, you will know:

  • Which channels are genuinely profitable vs. which are creating the illusion of performance
  • The true cost of acquiring a customer through each platform
  • Exactly which campaigns to scale, which to pause, and which to eliminate
  • A specific budget reallocation plan with projected impact on profit
Package 02 / 06

Customer Retention Audit

"I'm acquiring new customers — but they buy once and disappear. My repeat purchase rate is too low and I don't know how to fix it."

Acquiring a new customer costs 5–7x more than retaining an existing one. If your customers are buying once and not returning, you're on a treadmill — spending constantly on acquisition just to maintain flat revenue. This audit identifies exactly why customers aren't coming back — and gives you a precise plan to change that.

Investment$350 — $700
Timeline7–12 Days
Best ForRepeat purchase stores

What We Analyze

01

Customer Acquisition Quality Analysis

Not all new customers are equal. We analyze which acquisition channels bring customers who actually return vs. those who buy once and leave. This single insight often changes everything about how a business allocates its marketing budget.

02

Retention Rate Calculation

We calculate your 30-day, 60-day, and 90-day retention rates — and benchmark them against what healthy stores in your category typically achieve. You'll know exactly how far off you are and what closing that gap is worth in annual revenue.

03

Full RFM Customer Segmentation

We segment your entire customer base into 8 behavioral groups based on how recently they purchased, how frequently, and how much they spend. Each group receives a specific recommended action.

04

Churn Pattern Analysis

We identify the common behaviors and characteristics of customers who stop buying, and uncover the factors most associated with churn.

05

Customer Behavior Flow Analysis

We map what customers actually do between purchases — what they browse, what they add to cart without buying, and at what point in their post-purchase journey they go quiet.

06

Cohort-Based Retention Comparison

We compare retention rates across different customer acquisition cohorts — customers acquired in Q1 2024 vs. Q3 2024, for example — to identify whether your retention is improving, declining, or staying flat over time.

Deliverables & Promise

Customer Retention Report (7–10 pages)
Full analysis with segment-by-segment breakdown and findings.
Segmented Customer Database (CSV)
Every customer labeled by RFM segment and churn risk — ready to upload to Klaviyo or Mailchimp.
Churn Risk List
Specific customers identified as high-risk in the next 30 days — with recommended outreach approach for each segment.
Retention Strategy Recommendations
Email sequence recommendations, re-engagement offer ideas, and loyalty trigger points — tailored to each segment.
45-Minute Strategy Call + 2 Weeks Email Support

After this audit, you will know:

  • Which customers are about to leave — and how to reach them before they do
  • Which acquisition channels bring your most loyal customers (so you can invest more there)
  • Why your customers stop buying — and the specific interventions that will change that behavior
  • A segmented, upload-ready customer list so you can start your retention campaigns immediately
Package 03 / 06

Product Portfolio Audit

"I'm managing 50+ SKUs, my inventory costs are high, and I honestly don't know which products are worth keeping, scaling, or cutting."

Most e-commerce businesses carry far more products than they should — and invest in the wrong ones. Your best-selling product by revenue is often not your most profitable product. Your slowest-moving product is tying up cash that could be working harder elsewhere. This audit gives you complete clarity on your entire catalog so every inventory decision going forward is data-backed.

Investment$350 — $800
Timeline7–14 Days
Best ForLarge catalogs (50+ SKUs)

What We Analyze

01

ABC Product Classification

We classify every product in your catalog into three tiers — A-tier: High value, high priority (protect and grow); B-tier: Moderate value (optimize and monitor); C-tier: Low value, low velocity (reduce investment or discontinue).

02

Product-Level Profit Margin Analysis

We calculate the true margin on each product after deducting cost of goods, return costs, shipping costs, and proportional ad spend. The results consistently surprise business owners.

03

Category Performance Analysis

We identify which product categories are growing, which are declining, and which are flat — and whether the trend is accelerating or stabilizing.

04

Pricing Analysis

We assess whether your current pricing is leaving money on the table, whether certain products are underpriced relative to their perceived value, and where small price adjustments could meaningfully increase margin without hurting conversion.

05

Inventory Efficiency Analysis

We calculate how long your current stock will last based on actual sales velocity, which products are at risk of stockout, and which are grossly overstocked — tying up cash unnecessarily.

06

Dead Stock Identification

We identify products that have not moved within a defined period and calculate the true cost of holding them — and recommend strategies to liquidate or reactivate them.

07

Cross-Sell & Bundle Opportunity Analysis

Using market basket analysis, we identify which products your customers buy together most frequently — and design 5 specific bundle recommendations that could increase your average order value immediately.

ABC Classification Spreadsheet
Complete product list with tier assignments, margin data, and velocity metrics.
Dead Stock Report
Products to liquidate, with estimated holding cost and suggested clearance strategies.
Top 5 Bundle Recommendations
Specific product pairings with projected AOV increase for each.
Inventory Investment Priority List
Where to reorder immediately, where to reduce, and where to stop entirely.
Pricing Adjustment Recommendations
Specific products where a price change is supported by data.
45-Minute Strategy Call + 2 Weeks Email Support

After this audit, you will know:

  • Which products to invest in, which to optimize, and which to cut — backed by actual data
  • The true profit margin on every product in your catalog — not just revenue
  • Which inventory is costing you cash without generating return
  • Exactly which products to bundle together to increase average order value starting this week
Package 04 / 06

Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Audit

"I have traffic coming to my site — but very few people are actually buying. I know I'm losing sales somewhere, I just don't know where."

The average e-commerce conversion rate is 1.5%–3%. If yours is below that, you are leaving significant revenue on the table — from traffic you're already paying for. This audit maps every step of your customer's journey and identifies, with precision, where and why they're leaving without buying.

Investment$250 — $500
Timeline7–10 Days
Best For3,000+ sessions/mo

What We Analyze

01

Full Sales Funnel Drop-Off Analysis

We map every step: Landing Page → Product Page → Add to Cart → Checkout Initiated → Payment → Purchase Completed. We calculate the drop-off rate at every single transition and identify which step is costing you the most conversions.

02

Cart Abandonment Analysis

We analyze your cart abandonment rate broken down by device type, traffic source, product category, and time of day. Abandonment behavior is rarely random — patterns emerge quickly and point directly to fixable causes.

03

Checkout Drop-Off Analysis

Many businesses focus on cart abandonment but overlook checkout drop-off — which is often more severe. We examine every field, every step, and every friction point in your checkout flow.

04

User Journey Mapping

We trace the paths real visitors take through your site — where they enter, which pages they visit, where they hesitate, and where they exit. This reveals navigation problems and content gaps that are invisible without data.

05

Bounce Rate & Engagement Analysis

We analyze which pages visitors are landing on and immediately leaving — broken down by traffic source. A high bounce rate from paid traffic is expensive. We'll show you exactly where it's happening and why.

06

Mobile vs. Desktop Conversion Comparison

If your mobile conversion rate is significantly below your desktop rate, you have a specific, fixable problem. We identify precisely what is causing the mobile experience to underperform.

Deliverables & Promise

CRO Audit Report (6–9 pages)
Step-by-step funnel analysis with every drop-off point identified and explained.
Conversion Leak Map
Visual diagram showing exactly where visitors are leaving your site and why.
Prioritized Fix List
Every recommended change ranked by estimated conversion impact — highest ROI fixes first.
Device-Specific Recommendations
Separate action plans for mobile and desktop if performance diverges significantly.
Quick Win vs. Long-Term Fix Separation
Changes you can make this week vs. changes that require development work.
45-Minute Strategy Call + 2 Weeks Email Support

After this audit, you will know:

  • The exact pages and steps where your visitors are abandoning — and why
  • Which fixes will have the highest impact on your conversion rate
  • Whether your mobile experience is costing you significant revenue
  • A prioritized action list that could increase your conversion rate by 20–40% using your existing traffic — without spending a single additional dollar on ads
Package 05 / 06

E-Commerce Profitability Audit

“My revenue looks healthy and keeps growing — but my profit never seems to reflect that. I don't know where the money is going.”

Revenue growth is easy to celebrate. Profit is harder to find. Many e-commerce businesses discover — too late — that they've been scaling a fundamentally unprofitable operation. This audit builds your complete financial picture: where money comes in, where it quietly leaks out, and what to do about it.

Investment$350 — $700
Timeline8–12 Days
Best ForFlat/declining profit

What We Analyze

01

True Product-Level Profit Margin

We calculate the actual margin on every product after accounting for: Cost of Goods, Shipping Costs, Return & Restocking Costs, Platform Fees, and Proportional Ad Spend. The result is your real profit — not your accounting profit.

02

Cost Structure Analysis

We break down every cost category in your business and identify which costs are growing faster than your revenue — a reliable early warning sign of margin compression.

03

Revenue Trend vs. Profit Trend Comparison

We map both curves side by side. If revenue is climbing but profit is flat or declining, the gap between those two lines is the most important number in your business. We find it, explain it, and show you how to close it.

04

Discount & Promotion Impact Analysis

Discounts often feel like growth drivers but function as profit destroyers. We analyze every promotion you've run in the past 6–12 months — measuring the true net impact on profit after accounting for margin erosion, incremental volume, and new customer acquisition.

05

Cash Flow Pattern Analysis

We identify which months your cash position tightens and why — so you can plan inventory purchases, ad spend increases, and operational investments around your actual cash availability rather than your projected revenue.

06

Seasonal Profitability Analysis

We calculate not just when your revenue peaks — but when your profit peaks. These are often different months, and understanding the gap is critical for planning.

07

Break-Even Analysis

We calculate the minimum monthly revenue required to cover all fixed and variable costs — so you always know exactly how far above or below breakeven you are operating.

Deliverables & Promise

Profitability Audit Report (8–11 pages)
Complete financial picture with every finding clearly explained.
True P&L View by Product & Channel
Real profit breakdown — not just revenue.
Cost Leak Identification
Specific cost categories that are growing disproportionately — with recommendations to address each.
Promotion Performance Summary
Every major promotion evaluated for true net profit impact.
6-Month Cash Flow Pattern Map
Month-by-month cash position projection based on historical patterns.
Break-Even Dashboard
Simple visual showing your current position relative to breakeven at all times.
45-Minute Strategy Call + 2 Weeks Email Support

After this audit, you will know:

  • Your true profit margin — on every product, every channel, and every customer segment
  • Exactly where your revenue is leaking into cost rather than converting to profit
  • Whether your promotions are building your business or quietly eroding it
  • When your cash will be tight in the coming months — so you can plan ahead instead of reacting
Package 06 / 06

Operations Efficiency Audit

“My return rate is climbing, my inventory costs are out of control, and as we scale, our operations feel more chaotic rather than more efficient.”

Operational problems are profit problems in disguise. High return rates, stockouts, overstocked SKUs, slow fulfillment, and underperforming suppliers all have measurable financial costs — and most businesses don't quantify them until the damage is already significant. This audit surfaces every operational inefficiency and attaches a dollar value to each one.

Investment$400 — $800
Timeline10–14 Days
Best ForScaling physical products

What We Analyze

01

Demand Forecasting Analysis

We build a 90-day forward-looking demand projection for your top products — so your inventory purchasing decisions are driven by data rather than intuition. We flag products at high stockout risk and products likely to become overstocked.

02

Inventory Efficiency Analysis

We calculate sell-through rates, days of inventory on hand, and inventory turnover for every product tier — and identify where cash is unnecessarily tied up in slow-moving stock.

03

Dead Stock Analysis

We identify every product that has not sold within a defined period, calculate the true cost of holding that inventory (including storage, opportunity cost, and obsolescence risk), and recommend specific liquidation or reactivation strategies.

04

Order Fulfillment Analysis

We analyze your order processing time from purchase to dispatch — broken down by product type, supplier, day of week, and order volume. Fulfillment delays are one of the most common — and most preventable — causes of negative customer reviews.

05

Delivery & Shipping Performance Analysis

We examine delivery time, delivery reliability, and shipping cost as a percentage of revenue — segmented by geographic region and carrier. We identify where shipping costs are disproportionately high and where delivery performance is creating customer satisfaction problems.

06

Return & Refund Operations Analysis

We analyze return rates by product, category, and supplier — and calculate the true total cost of returns including reverse shipping, restocking labor, lost margin, and repeat-order impact. We then identify the specific root causes driving your return rate and provide targeted fixes.

07

Supplier Performance Analysis

We build a scorecard for every supplier — measuring on-time delivery rate, order accuracy, defect rate, and cost consistency. Underperforming suppliers are often invisible until they're quantified. We make them visible.

08

Geographic Sales & Fulfillment Analysis

We identify which regions generate the most revenue, which generate the most returns, and where fulfillment performance is weakest — so you can make informed decisions about carrier selection, warehouse positioning, and regional marketing investment.

Deliverables & Promise

Operations Efficiency Report (9–13 pages)
Complete operational analysis with every finding quantified in financial terms.
90-Day Demand Forecast (Top 20 Products)
SKU-level projections with reorder recommendations and timing.
Supplier Performance Scorecard
Every supplier rated across 4 key dimensions with recommended actions.
Return Rate Analysis & Fix List
Root causes identified + specific product-level and process-level recommendations.
Dead Stock Report with Liquidation Plan
Products to move, how to move them, and projected cash recovery.
Inventory Investment Priority Sheet
45-Minute Strategy Call + 2 Weeks Email Support

After this audit, you will know:

  • Which suppliers are creating operational and financial problems — and what to do about each one
  • How much inventory to order, for which products, and when — based on 90-day forward demand data
  • The true financial cost of your current return rate — and the specific fixes that will reduce it
  • Where cash is unnecessarily locked in dead or slow-moving stock — and how to free it
