What We Analyze · 4 Key Areas

Four focused analyses that surface where revenue is leaking.

01

Sales & Revenue Snapshot

We examine your last 90 days of revenue data — identifying growth trends, unexpected spikes or drops, and your top 10 performing products. You'll know exactly which direction your revenue is moving and why.

02

Conversion Funnel Audit

We map every step of your customer journey — from landing on your site to completing a purchase — and pinpoint exactly where visitors are dropping off. We compare mobile vs. desktop performance and identify your single biggest conversion leak.

03

Top Customer Identification

Using a simplified RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) framework, we identify your top 20% most valuable customers, flag those at risk of leaving, and give you a prioritized list of who needs your attention right now.

04

Profit Reality Check

Revenue is vanity — profit is sanity. We calculate the gross margin on your top 5 products and flag any that look like winners on the surface but are quietly draining your profit.

What We Analyze · Part 1 of 2

Cohorts, forecasts, and the truth about your profit. Includes everything in the Growth Package, plus 6 advanced analyses.

10

Complete Cohort Analysis

We track every monthly group of customers you've acquired over the past 12–24 months and measure how each cohort behaves over time — retention, revenue contribution, purchase frequency, and LTV progression. This reveals your best-performing customer acquisition periods and why.

"Your November 2023 cohort is your most valuable — here's what you did differently that month, and how to replicate it."
11

90-Day Sales & Demand Forecasting

We build a data-driven revenue forecast for the next 90 days, including SKU-level demand projections for your top 20 products, seasonal adjustments, and three scenarios: best case, expected, and conservative.

"You need to order X units of Product B within the next 10 days — stockout risk is high going into next month."
12

Profit Margin Deep Dive

We calculate the true profit on every product — after deducting cost of goods, returns, shipping, and ad spend. We then map profitability by customer segment and by marketing channel to reveal where your real money is being made.

"Your best-selling product is actually your third most profitable — this product deserves your growth investment."

What We Analyze · Part 2 of 2

A live analytics engine, plus promotion & return intelligence.

13

Live Custom KPI Dashboard

We design and build a fully customized live dashboard connected directly to your Shopify and GA4 data. It auto-refreshes daily and includes six tailored views: Revenue Pulse, Customer Health Monitor, Marketing Efficiency, Product Performance Matrix, Inventory Alerts, and Profit Tracker.

You will never again say "I don't know what's happening in my business."
14

Discount & Promotion Strategy Analysis

We analyze the true impact of every promotion you've run — measuring revenue lift against margin erosion and new customer acquisition. We calculate your optimal discount thresholds and build a Q4 promotional calendar with profit projections.

"Your 20% discount events are actually reducing net profit — a targeted 10% offer to specific segments would generate higher returns."
15

Return & Refund Pattern Analysis

We analyze return rates by product, category, and supplier — and calculate the true financial cost of returns including shipping, restocking, and lost margin. We then identify the specific fixes (product descriptions, sizing guides, quality improvements) that will reduce your return rate.

"A 2% reduction in your return rate saves $X annually — here are the three product pages to fix first."
