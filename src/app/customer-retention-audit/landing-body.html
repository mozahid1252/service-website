The Deep Dive

Six lenses on why customers leave — and what brings them back.

01. Customer Acquisition Quality Analysis

We analyze which acquisition channels bring customers who actually return vs. those who buy once and leave.

02. Retention Rate Calculation

We calculate your 30, 60, and 90-day retention rates and benchmark them against healthy stores in your category.

03. Full RFM Customer Segmentation

We segment your base into 8 behavioral groups based on Recency, Frequency, and Monetary value.

04. Churn Pattern Analysis

We identify the common behaviors and characteristics of customers who stop buying.

05. Customer Behavior Flow Analysis

We map what customers do between purchases — what they browse, cart without buying, and when they go quiet.

06. Cohort-Based Retention Comparison

We compare retention rates across acquisition cohorts to identify if retention is improving, declining, or flat.
