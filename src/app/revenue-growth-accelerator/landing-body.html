How is this different from the Health Check?
The Health Check is a rapid 5-day diagnostic focusing on immediate leaks. The Growth Accelerator is a deep 14-day analysis that includes customer segmentation (RFM), LTV modeling, channel profitability, and a comprehensive 30-day implementation roadmap.
What is the CSV export used for?
We provide a customer segment CSV that is formatted and ready to upload directly into Klaviyo or Mailchimp. This allows you to immediately start targeting your Champions, At-Risk, and Loyal customers with tailored messaging.
Does this include implementation?
The package includes a 30-day roadmap and 30 days of email support to guide your team through implementation. If you need hands-on implementation, we can discuss our retainer options.
Do you need access to our ad accounts?
Yes, read-only access to Facebook Ads, Google Ads, and email marketing platforms is required to perform the true channel profitability and LTV analyses.
