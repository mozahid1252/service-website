What We Review

7 Business Health Areas

Each area is reviewed at a high level to identify the most important signals and risks.

1

Revenue & Sales Health

Understand the direction and concentration of your business

We review your recent sales performance to understand the direction and concentration of your business.

We examine:

  • Revenue trend
  • Order trend
  • Average order value
  • New versus returning customer revenue
  • Top-performing products
  • Declining product signals
  • Revenue concentration
  • Significant growth or decline periods
  • Recent business performance
You will learn: Whether your revenue performance appears to be improving, stable, declining, or requires further investigation.
2

Profitability & Margin Signals

Identify obvious margin risks

Revenue does not always equal profit. We review the available cost and order data to identify obvious margin risks.

We examine:

  • Product cost or COGS
  • Gross-margin signals
  • Available contribution-margin signals
  • Discount impact
  • Refund and return impact
  • Shipping and payment-cost signals, where available
  • High-revenue but low-profit product signals
  • Potential margin leakage
You will learn: Which products or cost areas may be generating revenue while reducing actual profit.
Note: This is a high-level profitability check. Detailed product-level margin modeling is available through a deeper audit or custom scope.
3

Product Portfolio Health

Identify which areas appear strong, weak, or need deeper analysis

We review product performance to identify which areas appear strong, weak, or in need of deeper analysis.

We examine:

  • Bestselling products
  • Product sales trends
  • Low-margin signals
  • Low-sales-velocity signals, where suitable inventory data is available
  • Product revenue concentration
  • Product return and refund signals
  • Initial bundle or cross-sell signals
  • Product investment priorities

Initial product directions may include:

  • Grow - Maintain - Investigate - Reprice - Bundle - Reduce
Note: Full inventory, warehouse, supplier, and dead-stock analysis is not included in the Standard Health Check.
4

Marketing Health

Identify performance signals and possible efficiency concerns

We review marketing channels at a high level to identify performance signals and possible efficiency concerns.

We examine:

  • Meta, Google, Email, Organic, Direct, Referral
  • Channel spend, where available
  • Revenue by channel
  • Reported ROAS
  • CAC signals
  • Channel-level efficiency
  • Profitability signals, where reliable cost data is available
You will learn which channels appear to: Produce strong performance signals; Generate revenue without clear profit; Receive potentially inefficient spend; Require better measurement; Require a deeper specialist audit.
Note: Detailed campaign, creative, audience, placement, or daily media-buying analysis is not included.
5

Tracking & Data Health

Check data consistency for high-level decision-making

We check whether your available business and marketing data is sufficiently consistent for high-level decision-making.

We examine:

  • Shopify orders versus GA4 purchases
  • Shopify revenue versus available platform-reported revenue
  • Missing purchase signals
  • Duplicate conversion signals
  • Basic UTM and source-quality signals
  • Data-source consistency
  • Major tracking limitations
  • Data availability and completeness
You will learn: Which data source should be treated as the primary business baseline; Whether your available marketing data appears dependable; Where major tracking concerns may exist; Whether a deeper Tracking & Attribution Audit may be needed.
Note: Full GTM, GA4, Pixel, CAPI, server-side tracking, or technical implementation is not included.
6

Funnel & CRO Health

Identify major conversion warnings

We review the customer journey at a high level to identify major conversion warnings.

We examine:

  • Traffic
  • Product views
  • Add-to-cart activity
  • Checkout activity
  • Purchases
  • Mobile versus desktop performance
  • Main funnel drop-off signals
  • Landing-page signals
  • Cart and checkout warnings
  • Available conversion-rate signals
You will learn: Whether a significant conversion bottleneck may exist; Whether the initial concern appears to be traffic or on-site experience; Whether mobile performance requires attention; Whether a full E-Commerce CRO Audit may be appropriate.
Note: This is a high-level funnel diagnostic. It does not include a page-by-page CRO audit, UX redesign, copywriting, or development.
7

Customer & Retention Health

Understand new vs repeat purchase dependency

We review basic customer behaviour to understand whether your business depends primarily on new customers or has a meaningful repeat-purchase base.

We examine:

  • New versus returning customers
  • Repeat-purchase signals
  • Customer revenue concentration
  • High-value customer signals
  • Inactive or at-risk customer signals
  • Customer acquisition quality
  • Basic customer-value signals, where sufficient history is available
You will learn: Whether retention appears strong, weak, or unclear; Whether the business is highly dependent on new customer acquisition; Whether a valuable customer base appears to exist; Whether deeper retention or LTV analysis may be useful.
Note: Full RFM segmentation, cohort analysis, churn modeling, and email-platform segmentation are not included.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Standard and Deep Health Check?
The Standard Health Check is a rapid, high-level diagnostic designed to identify the most important business risks and priorities. The Deep Health Check includes more historical, product, channel, customer, funnel, and data analysis for larger or more complex businesses.
Is this a full CRO audit?
No. The Standard Health Check identifies high-level funnel and conversion risks. A full page-by-page CRO analysis is available separately through the E-Commerce CRO Audit.
Is this a full profitability audit?
No. It identifies important profitability signals and risks. Detailed product, channel, or contribution-margin modeling requires a deeper scope.
Will you fix the problems found?
The Standard Health Check provides diagnosis and priorities. Implementation is available separately through the E-Commerce Performance Improvement engagement.
Do you manage Meta or Google Ads?
No. The audit reviews marketing health at a high level. Daily campaign management and media buying are separate services.
Will Shopify, GA4, and advertising-platform numbers match exactly?
Not necessarily. Each platform uses different attribution rules and reporting methods. We identify the key differences and recommend an appropriate baseline.
What if some of my data is missing?
We document the limitation and reduce the confidence of the related finding. You will also receive a recommendation for the most important data improvement.
Can I purchase a specialist audit directly?
Yes. If you already know the problem, you may start directly with a relevant specialist audit. If you are unsure, the Standard Health Check is a suitable starting point.
How long does the audit take?
The Standard Health Check is delivered within 3-5 business days after the required data and access are received.
