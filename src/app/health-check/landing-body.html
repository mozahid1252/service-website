Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the Health Check take?
The entire diagnostic is completed within 5 to 7 business days from the moment we receive access to your data sources.
What tools do you need access to?
We need read-only access to your Shopify admin, Google Analytics 4, and your primary ad platforms (Facebook/Google Ads). All data is kept strictly confidential.
Is this suitable for a brand new store?
The Health Check is best suited for stores doing at least $5K/month in revenue, as we need 90 days of historical data to identify meaningful trends and leaks.
What format is the dashboard in?
We build your visual dashboard in Looker Studio, Power BI, or Excel depending on your preference and complexity needs. It uses simple color-coding: Green (good), Yellow (watch), Red (fix now).
