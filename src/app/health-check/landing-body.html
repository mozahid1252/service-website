Decision Guide

Which One Should I Choose?

Choose Standard if:

  • You need a fast diagnosis
  • You are unsure where the main problem is
  • Your data is mostly recent
  • You have one store and a simpler setup
  • You want to decide what to investigate next

Choose Deep if:

  • Your business has larger data volume
  • You have multiple marketing channels
  • You need 6-12 months of historical analysis
  • Revenue and profit do not appear to match
  • You need customer, product, channel, and funnel comparison
  • You are preparing for a major growth or investment decision

Still unsure? Contact us and we will recommend the appropriate level before starting.
