Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

How does communication work?
We primarily use Slack or email for day-to-day communication, with a guaranteed 48-hour response time. We also have a dedicated 45-minute Zoom strategy call once a month to review the big picture.
What counts as an "Ad Hoc Analysis"?
If you have a specific question like "Why did Product C sales spike last Tuesday?" or "What's the LTV of customers who used the SPRING20 code?", we will run a custom query and provide the answer. You get 4 of these per month.
Can I cancel after the 2-month minimum?
Yes. After the initial 2-month setup period, the retainer is month-to-month. You can pause or cancel with 15 days notice.
Do I need to have purchased a Tier package first?
Not necessarily, but it helps. The retainer is designed to maintain and build upon existing analytics infrastructure. If you don't have dashboards set up yet, we may recommend starting with Tier 2 or 3.
