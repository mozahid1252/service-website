Core Package · Step 03: Grow

Turn Business Data Into Consistent, Measurable Growth

Your business does not need another isolated report. It needs a reliable process for understanding what happened, identifying why it happened, deciding what matters next, and measuring whether the next action improved the business.

The E-Commerce Growth Intelligence Partnership provides ongoing analytical and strategic support across profitability, marketing, conversion, products, customers, retention, tracking, and growth priorities. Readiness

Is Your Business Ready?

If several of these apply, your business is likely ready for an ongoing growth partnership rather than another one-time report.

  • Your business has consistent sales and meaningful customer data
  • Revenue is growing but profit growth is inconsistent
  • Marketing platforms report numbers that are difficult to reconcile
  • CAC, margin, LTV, or conversion performance is unclear

  • Your business is ready to implement approved recommendations

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a Health Check first?
Usually, yes. The Health Check creates a baseline and identifies the most important priorities. If you already have a recent, reliable audit, we can review it before deciding whether a new Health Check is needed.
Is this a fixed package?
No. It is a custom monthly partnership. The scope depends on your business priorities, data, team, and selected support model.
Is this the same as a full-service marketing agency retainer?
No. The partnership focuses on analytics, performance interpretation, strategic prioritization, experimentation, and selected optimization support. Daily media buying and creative production are not automatically included.
Will you implement every recommendation?
No. The monthly scope will state which work is provided by MozIntelligence, which work is coordinated, and which work must be completed by your internal team or another specialist.
Is CRO implementation included?
Only when explicitly included in the approved monthly scope.
Is campaign management included?
No. Daily campaign management and media buying require a separate agreement.
Is dashboard development included?
KPI review may be included. New dashboard development, automated data connections, and data infrastructure require a separate scope.
Do you guarantee growth?
No. The partnership improves measurement, prioritization, execution quality, and learning. Commercial results also depend on product-market fit, pricing, traffic, implementation, seasonality, competition, and other factors.
Can we start with one workstream?
Yes. A partnership can begin with one priority area and expand as the business develops.
How often will we communicate?
The cadence is defined in the proposal. Depending on the support model, communication may be monthly, biweekly, or weekly.
What happens after the first three months?
We review the performance, remaining priorities, KPI direction, and future needs. The partnership may be renewed, expanded, reduced, or concluded.
Can we adjust the scope or workstreams over time?
Yes. As priorities shift, the monthly scope can be reviewed and adjusted. The partnership is designed to evolve with your business, available data, and current growth questions.
