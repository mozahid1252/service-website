The Deep Dive

Seven financial analyses that expose where your money really goes.

01. True Product-Level Margin

Actual margin after COGS, shipping, returns, platform fees, and proportional ad spend. Real profit, not accounting profit.

02. Cost Structure Analysis

Break down every cost category. Identify which costs are growing faster than revenue (margin compression).

03. Rev vs. Profit Trend

Map both curves side by side. If revenue climbs but profit is flat, we explain how to close the gap.

04. Discount & Promotion Impact

Analyze every promotion in the past 6–12 months. Measure true net impact on profit after margin erosion.

05. Cash Flow Pattern Analysis

Identify which months cash tightens and why, so you can plan inventory and ad spend around actual availability.

06. Seasonal Profitability

Calculate when revenue peaks vs. when profit peaks. These are often different months.

07. Break-Even Analysis

Calculate minimum monthly revenue required to cover all fixed and variable costs.
