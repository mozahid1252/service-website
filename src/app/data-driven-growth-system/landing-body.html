FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Do we need to purchase BigQuery or Looker Studio licenses?
We work within your existing stack. Google Looker Studio is free. BigQuery has a generous free tier, but for enterprise data volumes, a paid tier may be required. We will advise on the exact requirements during onboarding.
How is the live dashboard built?
We use Google Looker Studio connected via BigQuery and the Shopify API. The dashboard auto-refreshes daily. We include a training video so your entire team knows how to read and interact with it.
What is the 90-Day Forecast Document?
It's a comprehensive document containing revenue projections in three scenarios (best, expected, conservative), an inventory investment plan, and a cash flow outlook for the next quarter.
Can you integrate our specific ad platforms?
Yes. We standardize Google and Facebook Ads out of the box. If you use TikTok, Pinterest, or Snapchat Ads, we can integrate those via BigQuery transfer services as well.
